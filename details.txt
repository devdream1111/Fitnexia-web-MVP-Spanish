FITNEXIA	
Product Requirements Document
Marketplace platform to connect users, gyms, schools and fitness instructors.
Version: 1.0 Platform: Web + Mobile App (iOS / Android) Date: 2026

1. Product Vision
Fitnexia is a global marketplace that connects three types of players in the world of sports and physical education:

•Users / Athletes: people who want to find and book classes or coaches.
•Instructors / Teachers: professionals who offer individual or group classes.
•Gyms / Schools: institutions that manage their class offerings and schedules.


MVP's objective
The goal of the MVP is to validate that the three profiles can register, publish or search for services, and complete a booking with integrated payment — on the web and in the mobile app.


2. User profiles
2.1 User / Athlete
•Search for classes by sport, location, schedule availability, and price.
•Book in-person or online classes.
•Make payments within the platform.
•Leave verified reviews after attending a class.
•Check your booking history.
•Accumulate credits redeemable for classes.
•As a club member: pay your club membership fee and check your account statement from the app.
•As a club member: reserve courts and spaces at your club (football, padel, tennis, rugby).

2.2 Instructor / Teacher
•Create your professional profile with photo, bio, disciplines and certifications.
•Publish individual or group classes with schedule, format and price.
•Manage your schedule and availability.
•Receive payments automatically via the platform.
•View your confirmed bookings and revenue history.
•It has a "Available now" button.

2.3 Gym / Club / School
•Create a verified institutional profile.
•Add multiple instructors to your account.
•Publish and manage group classes with limited spaces.
•Manage schedules and occupancy from a control panel.
•Access basic metrics: bookings, revenue, attendance.
•Leave verified reviews about the professional you hired.
•Manage the membership register and collect membership fees via recurring debit.
•Define your own fee plans (monthly, quarterly, family) in your currency.
•It registers and manages courts and spaces, and handles reservations by shift.
•View collections: members up to date, delinquent members and monthly collection rate.

4. Main flows of the MVP
4.1 Registration and onboarding
1.The user opens the app or website for the first time.
2.Select your profile type: User, Instructor, Gym or School.
3.You can register with email + password or with Google.
4.Complete your basic profile (photo, name, disciplines).
5.You are redirected to your main screen according to your profile.

4.2 Class Search and Booking (User)
6.The user accesses the search engine.
7.Apply filters: discipline, location, date, price.
8.Select a class or instructor from the results.
9.View the full profile with reviews, hours, and price.
10.Click on 'Book'.
11.Select your payment method and confirm.
12.Receive confirmation via email and push notification.

4.3 Class Publication (Instructor / Institution)
13.The instructor accesses their panel and clicks on 'New class'.
14.Complete: name, description, discipline, modality, date/time, price and quota.
15.Publish the class — it becomes visible in the search engine immediately.
16.Receive notification every time someone makes a booking.

4.4 Payment and collection
17.The user chooses the payment model at the time of booking (per class, monthly or per period).
18.Payment is processed through Mercado Pago.
19.Fitnexia automatically retains its commission.
20.The net amount is transferred to the instructor or institution within the agreed period.
21.Both parties receive a receipt for the transaction.

4.5 Post-class review
22.48 hours after the class, the user receives an email/push inviting them to leave a review.
23.You can only do so if you attended (confirmed and paid reservation).
24.Rate from 1 to 5 stars and leave an optional comment.
25.The review appears on the instructor's profile with their updated average rating.

4.6 Collecting a membership fee from a member
26.The club creates its membership plans and invites its members via link, code, or bulk upload.
27.The member registers, becomes linked to the club, and chooses their membership plan.
28.Authorize automatic debit of the fee through Mercado Pago.
29.Each period, the platform automatically charges the fee and records the transaction.
30.The member receives the receipt. If the payment fails, it is retried and the member is notified to resolve the issue.
31.Fitnexia retains its commission or fee and transfers the net amount to the club.

4.7 Booking a court
32.The user or member opens the club and chooses the type of court (football, padel, tennis, rugby).
33.View the availability calendar and select court, date, time, and duration.
34.The system calculates the rate according to the time slot and whether you are a daily member.
35.Confirm and pay for the appointment, or it will be discounted if your plan includes it.
36.You will receive confirmation via push notification and email. Your appointment will be blocked on the club's calendar.


5. Business Rules

5.1 Commissions
Plan	Sports Commission	Observation
Basic Plan (Free)	15% per transaction	No fixed monthly cost
Pro Plan ($29/month)	8% per transaction	For individual instructors
Institutional Plan ($99/month)	5% per transaction	For gyms and schools

5.2 Cancellations
•Cancellation with more than 24 hours' notice: full refund to the user.
•Cancellation with less than 24 hours' notice: no refund (or according to the instructor's policy).
•If the instructor cancels a class: automatic refund to all users.

5.3 Reviews
•Only one review can be left per class attended.
•Reviews cannot be edited once published.
•Fitnexia may moderate and remove reviews that violate the terms of use.

5.4 Quotas and waiting list
•The maximum number of participants is defined by the instructor when creating the class.
•When the class is full, it displays 'Waiting List' instead of 'Book'.
•If a user cancels, the first person on the waiting list receives an automatic notification with 2 hours to confirm.

5.5 Loyalty program (credits)
●The user accumulates 1 credit for each class booked and paid for through the platform.
●Upon accumulating 10 credits, the user automatically receives a free class.
●The free class can be exchanged for any class on the platform up to $30 in value.
●Fitnexia covers the cost of the free class: the instructor charges normally and the platform does not charge a commission on that transaction.
●Credits expire 12 months after the last booking. If the user does not make a booking for 12 months, the accumulated credits are lost.
●The user receives a push notification and email 30 days before their credits expire.
●Credits are not transferable between users nor redeemable for cash.
●The credit balance is visible in the user's profile at all times.

5.6 Membership fees and late payments
●The club defines the amount, currency, and frequency of each fee plan.
●The fee is collected by recurring direct debit. If a payment fails, the member's status changes to pending and, after X days, to delinquent.
●The club sets how many days of grace it applies before marking a member as delinquent.
●Optional: a member in arrears may be barred from booking courts or entering the club until they settle their account.

5.7 Court reservations
●Each court has an operating schedule and a shift duration defined by the club.
●The price of the shift may vary depending on the time of day (peak hour / weekend) and membership status.
●The club defines its cancellation window and the refund policy for court slots.
●A confirmed appointment blocks that court in that lane for the rest of the users.

5.8 Quota-based revenue model (decision pending)
●To be defined: Fitnexia charges a commission for each fee collected, a fixed monthly fee to the club (SaaS), or a combination of both.
The class commission scheme (15% / 8% / 5%) is intended for class bookings, not membership fees. A separate scheme should be established for the club module.


6. Technical Requirements
6.1 Platforms
•Web: Responsive application compatible with Chrome, Safari and Firefox (latest 2 versions).
•Mobile app: iOS 15+ and Android 10+. React Native or Flutter is recommended to maintain a single codebase.

6.2 Authentication
•JWT (JSON Web Tokens) for sessions.
•OAuth 2.0 for login with Google.
•Passwords hashed with bcrypt.

6.3 Payments
•Integration with Mercado Pago.
•Webhook support for confirming payments and handling errors.
•PCI-compliant storage: do not store card data on our own servers.

6.4 Notifications
•Push: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for Android and iOS.
•Email: SendGrid or similar for transactional and reminder emails.

6.5 Geolocation
•Google Maps API or Mapbox for map search and display.
•Geocoding addresses when creating an institution profile.

6.6 File Storage
•Profile pictures and gallery: AWS S3 or similar (with CDN to optimize loading).
•Automatic compression of uploaded images.

6.7 Security
•HTTPS is required on all routes.
•Rate limiting on authentication endpoints.
•Server-side input validation (not just client-side).
•GDPR: Users can request the deletion of their account and data.

6.8 Performance
•Initial app loading time: less than 3 seconds on a 4G network.
•Pagination in listings with more than 20 results.
•Lazy loading of images.

2)F-13 · Clases Recurrentes — Especificación de flujo

Quién puede usarla
Instructor y Gimnasio/Colegio (mismo flujo para ambos).

Flujo de creación
1.El instructor accede a "Nueva clase" desde su panel.
2.Completa los datos habituales: nombre, descripción, disciplina, modalidad, precio, cupo.
3.En el campo Frecuencia activa la opción "Clase recurrente".
4.Selecciona uno o más días de la semana (ej: lunes y miércoles).
5.Define el horario — se aplica igual a todos los días seleccionados.
6.Sin fecha de fin: la serie se genera indefinidamente hasta que el instructor la pause o elimine.
7.Confirma → la serie queda publicada y cada instancia aparece visible en el buscador con su fecha y hora correspondiente.

Comportamiento de la serie
1. Cada clase de la serie es una instancia independiente en el calendario, pero todas comparten los mismos datos base (precio, cupo, descripción).
2. Los usuarios reservan y pagan por instancia, igual que una clase normal.
3. El buscador muestra cada fecha por separado (no "una entrada genérica").

Edición y modificación
Cuando el instructor edita una clase recurrente, la app le pregunta:
¿Qué querés modificar?
1. Solo esta clase → cambia únicamente esa instancia puntual.
2. Esta y todas las siguientes → aplica el cambio desde esa fecha en adelante.
⚠️ Las instancias pasadas nunca se modifican (son historial).

Pausa y eliminación
1. Pausar la serie: deja de generar nuevas instancias futuras. Las reservas ya confirmadas siguen en pie.
2. Eliminar la serie: cancela todas las instancias futuras sin reservas. Las que ya tienen reservas requieren cancelación manual (con reembolso automático según F-17).

Cancelación de una instancia con reservas
Aplica la regla general de F-17:
1. Si el instructor cancela → reembolso automático a todos los usuarios reservados.
2. Los usuarios reciben notificación push + email.

Notificaciones relevantes
Evento	A quién	Canal
Nueva instancia publicada (generación automática)	Nadie (silencioso)	—
Alguien reserva una instancia	Instructor	Push
Instructor modifica/cancela una instancia	Usuarios reservados	Push + Email
Instructor pausa o elimina la serie	Usuarios con reservas futuras	Push + Email

3)F-03 · Verificación de Perfil — Especificación de flujo

Quién aplica:
Instructores/Profesores e Instituciones (Gimnasios/Colegios). Los usuarios/atletas no se verifican.

Estados posibles de un perfil:
ESTADO	BUDGE VISIBLE	PUEDE PUBLICAR CLASES
No verificado	Sin badge	Si, con advertencia visible
Verificación pendiente	“en revisión”	Si
Verificado	Badge azul Fitnexia	Si
Rechazado	Sin badge	Si, puede reintentar


Flujo del instructor:
1.Completa su perfil básico y puede empezar a publicar clases inmediatamente — aparece como "No verificado" en su perfil público.
2.En su panel ve un banner persistente: "Verificá tu perfil para generar más confianza y aparecer mejor posicionado."
3.Hace clic en "Verificar perfil" → se abre el flujo de verificación.
4.Sube los dos documentos requeridos:
DNI o documento de identidad (foto frente y dorso).
Título profesional o certificación deportiva (foto o PDF).
5.Confirma el envío → su estado cambia a "Verificación pendiente".
6.Recibe email de confirmación: "Recibimos tu solicitud. Te avisamos en un plazo de X días hábiles."

Flujo del equipo Fitnexia (Panel Admin):
1.En el panel de administración (F-36) aparece una cola de solicitudes pendientes.
2.El admin ve: nombre del instructor, fecha de solicitud, documentos adjuntos.
3.Abre la solicitud y revisa los documentos.
4.Toma una decisión:

✅ Aprobar → el perfil recibe el badge verificado. El instructor recibe push + email.
❌ Rechazar → el admin escribe el motivo. El instructor recibe push + email con la razón y puede volver a intentarlo.

Comportamiento en el buscador:
1. Los perfiles verificados aparecen con badge azul visible en la tarjeta de resultados y en su perfil completo.
2. Los perfiles no verificados aparecen sin badge — sin ocultarlos ni penalizarlos explícitamente, pero el badge genera diferenciación visual clara.
3. Recomendación: en los filtros de búsqueda (F-08) agregar opción "Solo verificados" como filtro opcional.

Notificaciones:
EVENTO	CANAL
Solicitud recibida	Email al instructor
Perfil aprobado	Push + email
Perfil rechazado (con motivo)	Push + email
Recordatorio si lleva 7 días sin verificarse	Email (único)


Requerimiento técnico para el dev

1. Los documentos subidos se almacenan en S3 privado (no públicos, solo accesibles desde el panel admin).
2. No se muestran en ningún perfil público bajo ninguna circunstancia.
3. El campo verification_status en la base de datos admite cuatro valores: unverified / pending / verified / rejected.
4. El campo rejection_reason se guarda para historial interno aunque no se muestre completo al instructor.
